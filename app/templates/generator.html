How a draft is made

  1. Your role decides which metadata the generator can see.
  2. Columns are classified as time, measure, dimension or identifier — as hypotheses.
  3. The planner proposes KPIs, charts and filters, and lists every assumption.
  4. A validator checks every field, chart type and aggregation.
  5. Trusted templates draw the preview from synthetic numbers.
